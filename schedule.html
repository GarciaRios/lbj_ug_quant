Schedule

Fall 2026

The schedule is the main course hub. Readings refer to OpenIntro Statistics, 4th edition. Problem-set exercise numbers correspond to that edition.

Weekly rhythm: Monday lecture · Wednesday guided lab · Friday review, troubleshooting, and On Your Own support.

The schedule may be adjusted to match the pace and needs of the class.
